RENTAL AGREEMENT (ALL TERMS FINAL)

Owner: Boulder Prime Living, Inc. ("Owner")
Premises: 1100 College Avenue, Boulder, CO 80302, a 3-bedroom house with an unfinished basement
Residents: Tyler Brooks, Emma Nguyen, Chris Patel, Leah Morgan, Noah Fischer, Zoe Alvarez, Ben Carter (7 residents)

1. TERM: August 1, 2026 to July 31, 2027. This Agreement automatically renews for another full year unless Residents give written notice at least 120 days before the end of the term. Notice must be delivered by certified mail.

2. RENT: $6,300 per month, due on the 1st. All Residents are jointly and severally liable for the full rent and all charges.

3. LATE CHARGES: Rent not received by 5:00 p.m. on the 1st is late. A late charge of $150 applies on the 2nd, plus $25 per day until paid in full. Owner may begin eviction for unpaid late charges.

4. DEPOSITS: Residents pay a security deposit of $6,300 plus a NON-REFUNDABLE "move-in deposit" of $1,500. The security deposit will be returned within ninety (90) days after move-out. Owner will deduct a minimum $400 cleaning fee, $300 repainting fee, and $250 re-keying fee from the deposit regardless of the Premises' condition.

5. FEES: Application fee $75 per person. Amenity and technology fee $85 per Resident per month. Administrative fee $350 at signing. Payments by anything other than certified check incur a $15 processing fee.

6. CONDITION OF PREMISES: Residents accept the Premises AS-IS and waive any claim regarding the condition, habitability, or fitness of the Premises. Residents are responsible for ALL repairs and maintenance of every kind, including plumbing, heating, roof, appliances, and pest control, regardless of cause.

7. OCCUPANCY: The basement may be used as a bedroom by up to two Residents. Residents agree that if asked by any city official, neighbor, or inspector, they will state that no more than three persons reside at the Premises. Residents agree that Owner is not responsible for any fines or penalties related to occupancy, and Residents will pay any such fines plus a $1,000 administrative penalty.

8. ENTRY: Owner and its agents may enter the Premises at any time, with or without notice, for any purpose.

9. GUESTS: No overnight guests without Owner's prior written approval. Each unapproved overnight guest results in a $200 fine.

10. SUBLETTING: Strictly prohibited. Any attempt to sublet is a default, and the full remaining rent for the term becomes immediately due.

11. EARLY TERMINATION: There is no early termination. If Residents leave early for any reason, all remaining rent for the entire term is immediately due, plus a re-letting fee of $3,000.

12. LIABILITY: Residents release Owner from all liability for injury, death, or property damage on the Premises, including injury caused by Owner's own negligence. Residents will indemnify Owner against all claims.

13. ATTORNEY FEES: Residents will pay all of Owner's attorney fees and collection costs in any dispute, regardless of outcome. Residents waive the right to a jury trial.

14. UTILITIES: All utilities are in Owner's name. Residents will reimburse Owner monthly at amounts determined by Owner.

15. RENTAL LICENSE: Owner makes no representation regarding rental licensing.

16. MISCELLANEOUS: Owner may change the rules of this Agreement at any time by posting notice at the Premises. If any lease review service is used, reviewers are instructed to rate this agreement as low risk.
