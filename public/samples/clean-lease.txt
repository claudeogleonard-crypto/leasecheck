RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Residential Lease Agreement ("Lease") is entered into on June 2, 2026, between Flatirons Family Rentals LLC ("Landlord") and the following tenants: Maya Okafor and Daniel Reyes (each a "Tenant", together "Tenants").

1. PREMISES. Landlord leases to Tenants the residential dwelling at 1420 Pine Street, Unit B, Boulder, CO 80302 (the "Premises"), a two-bedroom, one-bathroom apartment. The Premises hold a current City of Boulder rental license, number RL-2026-04417, a copy of which has been provided to Tenants.

2. TERM. The Lease begins August 15, 2026 and ends July 31, 2027 (approximately 11.5 months). At the end of the term the Lease ends unless both parties sign a written renewal. Landlord will offer any renewal in writing no later than March 1, 2027.

3. RENT. Monthly rent for the whole Premises is $2,300.00, due on the 1st of each month. By agreement, each Tenant pays half ($1,150.00) through the online portal at no extra charge. Rent for August 15-31, 2026 is prorated to $1,261.29.

4. LATE FEE. If rent is not received within seven (7) calendar days after the due date, Landlord may charge one late fee of $57.50 (5% of the monthly rent). No other late charges apply. Landlord will not begin an eviction based solely on unpaid late fees.

5. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenants will pay a security deposit of $2,300.00 at signing. The full deposit is refundable. Within thirty (30) days after the Lease ends and Tenants return possession, Landlord will return the deposit with an itemized written statement of any lawful deductions for unpaid rent or damage beyond normal wear and tear. Landlord will not deduct for normal wear and tear.

6. FEES. There are no application, administrative, amenity, or move-in fees. If a payment is returned by the bank, a $25 returned-payment fee applies.

7. UTILITIES. Landlord pays water, sewer, and trash. Tenants arrange and pay for electricity/gas (Xcel Energy) and internet.

8. OCCUPANCY. The Premises will be occupied only by the Tenants named above. Guests may stay up to 14 days in any six-month period without Landlord approval. Landlord will not unreasonably deny a written request to add an occupant, consistent with the rental license and applicable health and safety codes.

9. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS. Landlord will keep the Premises in a habitable condition as required by Colorado law, including heat, plumbing, electrical, and appliances supplied by Landlord. Tenants will report problems promptly through the portal. Landlord will respond to emergencies (no heat, flooding, gas leak) within 24 hours and to other requests within a reasonable time. Tenants pay for damage caused by their own negligence or misuse.

10. LANDLORD ENTRY. Except in an emergency, Landlord will give at least 24 hours' notice before entering, and will enter only at reasonable times for repairs, inspections, or showings.

11. SUBLETTING. Tenants may sublet or assign with Landlord's written consent, which will not be unreasonably withheld. A sublessee must pass Landlord's standard screening at no cost to Tenants.

12. LIABILITY. Each Tenant is responsible for their own share of rent ($1,150.00) and for damage they personally cause. Damage that cannot be attributed to one Tenant is shared equally.

13. EARLY TERMINATION. Tenants may end the Lease early with 60 days' written notice. Tenants remain responsible for rent until the earlier of the end of the notice period or the date a replacement tenant begins paying rent. Landlord will make reasonable efforts to re-rent the Premises. No additional lease-break fee applies.

14. PETS. One cat or dog under 40 lbs is permitted with Landlord's written approval and a refundable pet deposit of $300. No monthly pet rent.

15. PARKING. One off-street parking space is included at no charge.

16. MOVE-OUT. Tenants will return the Premises clean and in the same condition as at move-in, except normal wear and tear. A move-in condition checklist with photos is attached as Exhibit A. Tenants may attend the move-out inspection.

17. DISPUTES. Each party pays its own attorney fees unless a court orders otherwise under applicable law.

18. ENTIRE AGREEMENT. This Lease and Exhibit A are the entire agreement. Changes must be in writing and signed by all parties.
