STUDENT HOUSING LEASE

Landlord/Manager: Hill Properties Management Co.
Property: 925 14th Street, Boulder, CO 80302 (four-bedroom house)
Tenants: Jordan Lee, Priya Natarajan, Sam Whitfield, Ava Castillo

1. TERM. August 20, 2026 through August 10, 2027.

2. RENT. Total rent is $4,600 per month, due on the 1st. Rent must be paid as one payment for the whole house. Tenants are responsible for collecting shares among themselves.

3. JOINT AND SEVERAL LIABILITY. All Tenants are jointly and severally liable for all obligations under this Lease, including the full rent and any damages, regardless of which Tenant caused them or failed to pay.

4. LATE FEES. Rent received after the 7th day of the month is late. A late fee of $50 will be charged on the 8th day of the month.

5. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenants will pay a security deposit of $4,600. The deposit will be returned within sixty (60) days after move-out, less deductions as permitted by law. Carpet cleaning costs may be deducted if carpets are left soiled beyond normal wear and tear.

6. FEES.
   a. Application fee: $50 per applicant.
   b. Lease administration fee: $200 (non-refundable), due at signing.
   c. Utility/Trash service fee: $95 per month, flat, regardless of usage, added to rent.
   d. Lease renewal fee: $150.

7. UTILITIES. Tenants pay electricity, gas, and internet directly. Water, sewer, and trash are covered by the Utility/Trash service fee in Section 6(c).

8. AUTOMATIC RENEWAL. This Lease will automatically renew for an additional 12-month term at the then-current market rent unless Tenants give written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the term.

9. MAINTENANCE. Landlord will make repairs to major systems. Tenants are responsible for minor repairs under $150 per occurrence, including clogged drains, light fixtures, and appliance repairs, as well as yard maintenance and snow removal from sidewalks.

10. ENTRY. Landlord may enter the Premises with 24 hours' notice for any reasonable purpose, and without notice in an emergency.

11. OCCUPANCY AND GUESTS. Only Tenants named in this Lease may occupy the Premises. No guest may stay more than 7 consecutive nights or more than 14 nights total in any calendar year without written approval. Occupancy of this property may not exceed the number of unrelated persons permitted by the City of Boulder land use code.

12. SUBLETTING. No subletting or assignment without Landlord's written consent. Each approved sublease is subject to a $300 transfer fee.

13. EARLY TERMINATION. If Tenants terminate early, Tenants owe rent until the Premises are re-rented, plus a re-letting fee of $750. Landlord will make reasonable efforts to re-rent the Premises.

14. ATTORNEY FEES. In any legal action under this Lease, the prevailing party may recover reasonable attorney fees and costs.

15. PETS. No pets.

16. PARKING. Two driveway spaces are available. Parking on the lawn is prohibited; violation fee $50 per occurrence.

17. MOVE-OUT CLEANING. The Premises must be returned in professionally clean condition.
